Knit your own beanie

A beginner pattern for the Drystone beanie, with a yarn table.

30 August 2026

You will need

• 4 mm circular needle
• 4 mm double-pointed needles
• A stitch marker
• A darning needle

Yarn

Size      Head (cm)    Yarn (g)
Small     52           70
Medium    56           85
Large     60           100

To print the pattern, press Alt+Shift+P.
